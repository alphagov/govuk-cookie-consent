Choose which cookies we use on GOV.UK

Cookies are files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website.

We use cookies to collect and store information about how you use the GOV.UK website and government digital services, such as the pages you visit. 'Government digital services' means any page with service.gov.uk in the URL.

Some cookies are necessary to make our website work. You can choose which other cookies you're happy for us to use. Read our detailed cookie information to find out which cookies we use and how long we keep them for.

Strictly necessary cookies

These essential cookies do things like remember your progress through a form (for example a licence application). They always need to be on.

Cookies that measure website use

We collect some information about how you use GOV.UK so that we can improve the website. You cannot be identified from the data.

We use Google Analytics cookies to collect information about things like:

  • how you got to GOV.UK
  • the pages you visit on GOV.UK
  • what you click on during your visit

We use Google Analytics cookies that measure general website use, for example what the most visited pages are. You can opt out of these.

We also gather more detailed data to understand how people use GOV.UK. We do this only if you opt in for cookies that allow more detailed website analysis.

We also use LUX Real User Monitoring software cookies from SpeedCurve to measure how well the website performed on your device and if there were any functionality errors. We only use these cookies if you opt in for more detailed website analysis.

We do not allow Google or SpeedCurve to use or share this data for their own purposes.
